## What it covers (from upstream)

Emil Kowalski–inspired animation patterns for Next.js/React with 8 working
examples: hover micro-interactions, toast notification entrance/exit, character
and word text reveals, modal transitions, staggered text, toast stacking,
layoutId morphs, iOS card expansion. Motion that feels inevitable and
purposeful rather than decorative.

Complements the vendored `emilkowalski-motion/` (the canonical source for this
philosophy). Revisit if upstream adds a license.
