## What it is

Garry Tan's open-source "software factory": 23 opinionated slash-command tools
that turn Claude Code into a virtual engineering team — a CEO who rethinks the
product, an eng manager who locks architecture, **a designer who catches AI
slop**, a reviewer who finds production bugs, a QA lead driving a real browser,
a security officer running OWASP + STRIDE audits, and a release engineer who
ships the PR. All Markdown, all free.

## Why it's on Unslop's shelf

The designer role explicitly hunts AI slop — same enemy as Unslop, different
angle (agentic review vs. component library). Worth studying for Unslop's audit
and design-intel work.
