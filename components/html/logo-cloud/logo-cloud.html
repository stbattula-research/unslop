The gallery wall · on view now

Hung at eye level.

Every logo on this wall earned its frame. None of them were shrunk to apologize for themselves.

Curator's note — most logo clouds are a grey strip of thumbnails begging you not to look. These are hung like paintings: one frame each, room to breathe, a plaque that says what it is. If a logo can't hold a wall, it doesn't belong on one.

A logo cloud is a trust section. Trust is not built by shrinking eight marks until nobody can read them.
